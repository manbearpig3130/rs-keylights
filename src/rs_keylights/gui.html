    Messages

      Default tones

      For parts without tones of their own, playing a song or following the game.

      Guitar

      Guitar pedal

      A Harley Benton DNAfx GiT's presets, switched over its USB cable (with dnafx-preset). On the Launchkey, knob 6 steps through them (knob 8 ten at a time while one shows), and the pad under it goes through your favourites.

      Other MIDI devices

      A kind of part can sound on another MIDI device (a synth, with its own speakers) instead of the keyboard, which still lights its keys. It keeps its own sound: no tones are sent to it.

      Tone changes

      When a song switches tone (clean, distortion, acoustic…, from the tone's name or its pedals), the part's tone switches to the one for that kind here; back to the song's first tone, to the part's own. Palm-mute and low-note tones stay as they are.

      Launchkey keys

      Its keys play the Casio (on channel 1, a part of their own) and light the keys they play: first, a song's lights getting the rest until you let go. On the Launchkey, knob 7 steps through the tones (knob 8 through their groups while one shows), and the pads under it turn them on and off and choose whether they light the keys.
